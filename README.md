# Student Resume Builder

A free, browser-based student resume builder for creating a clear, professional, and ATS-friendly resume.

**Live website:** [Open the Student Resume Builder](https://haseebullah-rassoli.github.io/student-resume/)

## About

Student Resume Builder helps students, recent graduates, scholarship applicants, and entry-level job seekers organize their education, projects, skills, experience, and achievements into a professional resume.

The tool combines guided resume writing prompts with a live preview, making it easier to prepare a student CV, scholarship resume, internship resume, or entry-level job application.

## Features

- Live resume preview while you enter your information
- ATS-friendly, single-column resume structure
- Sections for education, projects, skills, experience, volunteering, and certifications
- PDF and Word document export
- Practical resume-writing guidance and example phrases
- Browser-based use with no account required
- Mobile-friendly interface
- Resume information remains in your browser and is not uploaded by the application

## Who It Is For

- High school and university students
- Recent graduates
- Scholarship applicants
- Internship applicants
- Entry-level job seekers
- Anyone preparing a simple student resume or CV
