Student Resume Builder

Create a clear, ATS-friendly student resume for job applications, internships, or scholarships. Organize your education, projects, skills, and experience, then download your resume as a PDF or Word file.

Keep this in the main body — never in a header or footer (Chapter 4.9).

Suggested Phrases & Vocabulary

Pulled directly from Chapters 5 and 9. Click "Copy" and paste into the form above — then edit the specifics so every line honestly reflects your own experience.

Objective Formula (no work history yet)

Recent [Degree] graduate with hands-on experience in [skill 1] and [skill 2] through coursework and [project/internship]. Seeking an entry-level [target role] to apply [1–2 key strengths].

Example:
Recent Computer Science graduate with hands-on experience in Python and SQL through coursework and personal projects. Seeking an entry-level Data Analyst role to apply strong analytical skills.

Project Bullet Formula

[Action Verb] + [what you built/did] + [tool(s) used] + [result, if measurable]

Example:
Built a personal finance tracker using Python and SQLite, reducing manual budget tracking time by an estimated 3 hours per month.

Action Verbs for Students

Projects: Built, Designed, Developed, Created, Programmed
Coursework/Research: Analyzed, Researched, Presented, Investigated
Teamwork: Collaborated, Contributed, Coordinated, Assisted (with a specific result)
Leadership (clubs/teams): Organized, Led, Founded, Managed

Words & Phrases to Avoid

Avoid: "Hardworking", "Fast learner", "Team player" (with no example),
"Responsible for...", "Helped with...", listing coursework with no
context for why it matters to the target role.

Replace with a specific project, tool, or result instead.
